Preliminary Findings: NYC E-Scooter Crashes (2021–2025)

Main Finding

Brooklyn recorded the highest number of e-scooter crashes among collisions with a recorded borough between 2021 and 2025, with 1,682 unique collisions.

Data Coverage

The analysis identified 6,363 unique collisions involving e-scooters.

Of these, 4,890 had a borough recorded in the dataset and were included in the borough comparison. Another 1,473 unique collisions had no borough recorded and were excluded from the ranking.

Limitation

Because some collision records did not include borough information, the borough-level results do not represent a complete count of all e-scooter crashes in New York City.

These findings are preliminary and are based on the available records in the NYC Open Data datasets.
